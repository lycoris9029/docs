> **Documentation status:** This page describes the inspected backend source, not a verified production deployment. Implemented behavior and recommended changes are explicitly distinguished.

The service uses stateless Spring Security filter-based authentication and Spring method security. `TokenProvider` creates signed JWTs, `TokenFilter` validates requests, and the `@el.check(...)` expression evaluates permissions such as `librarySeat:add` and `libraryYuyue:list`. Passwords are hashed with `BCryptPasswordEncoder`.

- **Public endpoints:** `GET /auth/code`, `POST /auth/login`, `DELETE /auth/logout`, root health text and selected documentation/static paths.
- **Login protection:** arithmetic captcha challenge stored in Redis; encrypted credential submitted in the login request.
- **Authenticated endpoints:** require JWT even when they have no `@PreAuthorize` method annotation.
- **Permission controls:** most administrative and library CRUD operations enforce named permissions.
- **Online sessions:** session/token information is also maintained through the online-user service and Redis utilities.

**Current risk:** Some mutation endpoints (`/api/libraryYuyue/li`, `/dao`, `/tui`) have no explicit method-level ownership/role checks and accept reservation IDs from the client. JWT authentication alone is not proof that a caller owns the referenced reservation.
